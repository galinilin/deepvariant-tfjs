deepvariant · in-browser

Variant calling, locally.

Google's DeepVariant 1.8 InceptionV3 model running on your GPU via TensorFlow.js. Encoded pileup → softmax over hom_ref · het · hom_alt.

DeepVariant is a deep-learning variant caller. Given a candidate position in a genome, it builds a 100×221×7 "pileup image" of the reads spanning that position and feeds it through an InceptionV3 CNN that outputs P(hom_ref · het · hom_alt).

This site runs the released DV 1.8 WGS model end-to-end in your browser: the encoder, the candidate engine, and the model. No server. No data leaves your machine.

Synthetic generates a 1500 bp reference + scenarios (het, hom_alt, deletion, insertion, low-quality variants…) so you can see the encoding shapes directly. Real BAM loads ~2,500 actual NA12878 reads + 43 DV-emitted candidates from a 5 kb slice of chr20 in DV's own testdata — Prev/Next steps through real variants.

Source: deepvariant-tfjs · model conversion: dv-tfjs · upstream: google/deepvariant
